About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Biostatistics at Schulich School of Medicine & Dentistry, University of Western Ontario (UWO), under the supervision of Dr. Osvaldo Espin-Garcia and Dr. Yun-Hee Choi. I completed an MSc in Biostatistics at Western and an Honours Bachelor of Science degree at the University of Toronto with majors in Mathematics and Statistics. My research interests combine methodology development and applications, especially two-phase sampling for next generation sequencing data, missing data in family-based studies, statistical genomics, clustered data, and applied health data science. I enjoy collaborating with multidisciplinary scientists to deliver effective and statistically grounded solutions. I find pleasure in solving biostatistical challenges and have a long-term objective to secure an academic role, collaborating with diverse professors and industries.
